A new clip cannot be picked up without restarting the TTS server, because KoboldCpp scans the voices folder once at startup. That takes about ten seconds and the page will say so while it happens.

For the clone to be any good, record 3–10s of one speaker only, no music or echo, natural connected speech with some pitch movement. The clip is the entire model of your voice — nothing is trained.
